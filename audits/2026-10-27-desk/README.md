# The desk audit, 26 to 27 October 2026

Anthony asked for an overnight audit of Job Admin & Inv from the Transition Concierge's seat, then widened it: *"pay extra attention to the desk job … how we get items or approvals to trust officers or executors, how we value things, how we create the inventory … I want agents that can value things and identify things better than anyone else … a highly, highly accurate list of contents and valuations to a discerning trust attorney or executor."* Nothing in the app was changed.

- `PLAN.md`: the upgrade plan. Verdict, Fix now, Best in class, 33 build packs in four phases, questions for Anthony, what works, and the appendix mapping every finding to its pack (with its recheck status on `main` at 7abc836).
- `research-standards.md`: Florida §733.604 and Rule 5.340, Chapter 736, Treas. Reg. §20.2031-1(b) and -6, USPAP 7 and 8, what counsel and trust officers expect, failure modes. Each claim marked [P] primary, [S] secondary or [U] unconfirmed: page fetches failed in the build environment, so every claim rests on search extracts.
- `research-market.md`: the Palm Beach competition, price-data sources and their terms, what vision models get wrong, a ranked best-in-class list. Same marks, same caveat.
- `findings/<LENS>.json`: the twelve lenses (EST, VAL, REL, LIV, FLD, NEW, ACC, DOC, INV, AGT, ROW, SYN), each finding with the independent verifier's verdict, re-rated severity, note and reproduction. `GAPS-and-REDTEAM.json`: the three gap audits (the Ellison china on its real record, the pour-over estate, the Snapshot wipe) and the two red-team reviews. `RECHECK-7abc836.json`: every fix-now finding rerun on `main` after the flagged-only review and the DocuSign release request landed (99 still, 3 changed, 2 fixed).

Method: each lens drove the real page in headless Chromium on a frozen copy of `main` (3f25460) with a stubbed Apps Script and the agents answered from a catalogue, and read the code and the agents' prompts; the real agents could not be called (no Anthropic key in the build environment). An independent verifier reproduced every finding before it was kept. Evidence paths name the session's scratchpad, which does not survive the session; the reproductions are described in each finding. Drive ids are redacted.
