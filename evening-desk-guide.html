The Evening Desk

The Job Admin & Inv tab after a day in a client’s house: work down from the top, and stop when the bars are grey.

How it works

The tab runs in the order of an evening: what is urgent and the money step at the top, tonight’s items in the middle, four bars at the foot.

Rule 1

Red first, then money, then items

The bars last.

Rule 2

The row is the work

An item’s row holds its name, where it goes, who takes it and its value. Open its ▾ record only for the exceptions.

Rule 3

Grey can wait

Amber needs you tonight; red needs you now. Grey is for your information.

Before you sit down

  • If a room on the Job Plan says ⚠ not saved, tap Retry there first.
  • Leave field mode (Field · exit): it hides this tab.
  • On a phone the timeline under the brown band folds to one summary (Timeline · 3 of 8 done); tap it to open. The selection bar shrinks to 2 selected, Actions ▾ and Clear, its buttons under Actions.

1. The top of the page

The schedule says where the job stands: Working day 6 of 14 · 40% of the work done.

Anything urgent

Under it, only when something cannot wait, a strip of warnings, each saying what to do. Each goes once it is dealt with.

  • CALL ANTHONY IMMEDIATELY after an injury, a missing item or a worry about an older person: make the call, then press I called Anthony (Reporting an Incident). An open injury or hazard stays red until resolved; other open incidents show in amber.
  • An original will or cash found on site, until it is handed over and receipted, and a will until it is deposited with the clerk: Open Found on site.
  • A key or access item marked lost: Open Keys & access.
  • Firearms flagged on this job — photograph in place, nothing moves, where intake flagged firearms, with a link to the Protocol.
  • An incident still owing one of its promises, red once its day has passed: Open Incidents.
  • In amber, an estate with no matter type recorded: set it in Edit Client.

The brown band

One step and one filled button, such as ✉ Send midpoint invoice. The answer to a send or a filing appears in the band, so look up after you press.

Must find

Press Found it as the photos turn each one up. Once all are found the list shrinks to ✓ All 3 must-finds found; Show found brings it back.

2. Job Admin

Job Admin — desk paperwork is the closing checklist. Leave it shut mid-job: anything urgent in it is already in the strip above. Open it at the close: it lists what the app recorded by itself (rooms cleared, hours, the final invoice, the signed papers) and the few boxes only a person ticks. On Home Prep it starts open.

3. Tonight’s items

Always open. The Inventory Review bar carries the buttons in the order you press them. The list opens on Today when something was photographed today.

  1. Name N shots. Agent One names each photo. The room check follows by itself and flags anything photographed twice in a room; Check N rooms runs it again.
  2. Agent One’s notes, under Agent One raised 3 things to look at. Must find, Firearm and Possible NFA item in red, and they stay (a Must find goes quiet once every must-find is found; a Firearm note outlives the item it was raised on); Look at this in amber, gone once you review the item. Quiet notes stay in the item’s record. Show the item jumps to it and opens it.
  3. Possible duplicates. Compare the photos, then Remove the extra, or Not duplicates if they really are two things. Ticking Reviewed, or renaming one, does not settle it.
  4. The values follow by themselves, Agent Two correcting Agent One’s guesses; Value N lines does the rest.
  5. Not yet decided, room by room. Correct the name and count, choose where it goes, type who takes it if it leaves, check the value against what you saw. Nothing flagged? No tick, whatever its value: the box is ticked in grey. Flagged? One Reviewed tick covers it: a note, a firearm or ammunition, an appraisal not yet in or waived, a bequest, a dispute, both agents unsure. Hover the box for every reason. A tick never settles an item with no name or a possible duplicate (the box turns bronze): name it, or Remove or Not duplicates. On a disputed estate every item either agent named or valued takes it. Bronze unsure marks any guess, even on a grey tick, until your tick or a value you type: read those hardest. A maker added to the name reprices it.
  6. A drawer photographed piece by piece. Tick the rows (or Select all on the group) and a selection bar appears at the foot. Remove lines takes them off the list and keeps the photos; Set disposition… gives them all one destination, such as Donate.
  7. The Needs you counts cover every day. While Today is on, the filter row names what earlier days left undecided (1 from earlier days still undecided: press All): press All and finish them.
  8. Last, read the whole list against what you saw and press ✓ I’ve read the list on the Inventory Review bar: it records who and when, for the list as it stood. Change a name, category, count, value, destination, recipient, condition or room after, or remove an item, on any device, and it says changed since: press it again. A tick changes nothing. While the list is loading or the sheet is unreachable, the press waits.

4. The four bars

Each bar says in a few words whether anything inside needs you. It opens by itself when its work is due and never shuts by itself; what you open or shut stays that way until you reload.

  • Grey for your information
  • Amber needs you
  • Red needs you now
  • ✓ Green all done

Releases & signed papers

Opens by itself when items wait for written approval that no request has carried (3 items need written approval: send a request), when a drafted request waits for I’ve sent it, or when a signed approval has no copy filed.

  • Approval request. Send request makes a Gmail draft with the request as a PDF: to every representative with an email, the estate attorney copied, or to the client on living work. Send it from Gmail, then press I’ve sent it. From then on Record the signed request waits here for the signed copy.
  • Leaving the property · sign out on the iPad. One row for each selling vendor, charity or appraiser taking things away, and on an estate each person, with the item numbers. Sign out N to … opens the sheet; they sign with a finger and Sign and file puts it in Drive. Back from an appraiser: Check N back in from …. On an estate, Print receipt prints a paper receipt for a person instead.
  • Signed release approvals. One row for each day signatures were recorded, with File signed copy.
  • Beneficiaries & specific bequests, on an estate: the list counsel gives you (+ Add a beneficiary, + Add a specific bequest). When a promised item turns up, type its number and press Match.

Values & appraisals

Opens by itself when an item needs an appraisal and has no appraiser linked. Red on a Formal job, whose record is not complete without it; amber otherwise.

  • + Add an appraiser, from the Vendor Directory or typed in.
  • Link each item you send to an appraiser, or Waive (it asks who decided, and why). On every job the appraiser is then its source: Appraisal · their name · pending. Handing it over is a sign-out, under Releases.
  • When the report comes back, type its figure in Total: that is the appraisal, and the item leaves this list. Then File the report on the appraiser’s row (a PDF or a photo, to the client’s Drive). Until then the row says report not filed.
  • On an estate whose papers state values: the valuation basis and the date values are as of.

Closing the job

Grey before the close; amber mid-job only when something has left with its paper owed (a donated item with no receipt, a sold item on no statement) or a statement waits to be filed. Opens by itself at the close (the Job Plan at Close-out, the work handed over, the job closed or lost), when Close job refuses, or when a proceeds statement disagrees with its items (red).

  • The satisfaction call (a call, not a text), ✉ Draft review request, the referral ask, and stars for every vendor used.
  • The Disposition Ledger on an estate, the Contents Record on living work: print it, have it signed, then File signed copy. On an estate whose papers state values, the signed Court Inventory or Trust Schedule is filed here too.
  • Proceeds statements: + Record a statement, then File the statement. Donations: File charity receipt for each charity.

Close job, in the band, refuses until every vendor used is rated, every key is returned or recorded lost, and no incident is open. It names, without refusing, an unsigned ledger, rooms not cleared and items leaving with nobody named.

Removed items & snapshots

Opens by itself only when you take a snapshot. Restore gives back anything taken off the list. The totals (Show summary & rollups, in More ▾) appear under this bar, at the very foot.

Estate, living work, Home Prep

An estate

  • Every representative signs every release; each item released to a person needs a signed receipt.

Living work

  • The client approves what leaves, on the same request. No receipts from a person, no bequests.

Home Prep

  • No items and no bars: the top of the page (the band says ▶ Book the vendors until every vendor is confirmed), Job Admin open, and the close-out under it.

The week of a job

The night the signed request comes back

Releases & signed papers

  • Record the signed request opens on the items that request carried and that still wait for a signature, each ticked. Untick any the signed copy struck out, tick who signed, set the date on the paper, press Record approval, then File the signed request.
  • Only one of two representatives signed? Record it anyway. Those items stay on the next request until everyone has signed.

A pickup day

In the house, on the iPad

  • Out of field mode, open Releases. Sign out N to …, hand over the iPad, Sign and file. Or Photograph a signed sheet instead.
  • On an estate only items with everyone’s written approval are offered. A firearm never goes on a sheet.

The closing evening

Closing the job opens by itself

  • Work down Closing the job, then press Close job in the band. Closing files the closing papers to Drive by itself.

Rules that never bend

  • Nothing leaves an estate without written approval from every representative. Verbal approval never counts. Anything that left early goes on the next request for ratification, approved after the fact; it is never undone.
  • Firearms: nothing moves without written authority, and none goes on a sign-out sheet. Only Anthony drives one to a licensed dealer, never an NFA item (Firearms Protocol).
  • Havellin’s people never buy or receive estate property. The app refuses it on an estate; in a client’s own home it is flagged for the client to initial.
  • No item or paper is deleted. Remove line keeps the photo; a paper filed in error is voided with a reason.

Tricky spots

An item jumps away when I choose its destination.
It moves into its group. Keep working Not yet decided from the top; it shrinks as you go.

Tonight, in order

  1. Red first. Then the brown band’s one step.
  2. Found it for each must-find the photos turn up.
  3. Name N shots; the values follow. Then Possible duplicates.
  4. Not yet decided, room by room: name, where it goes, who takes it, value; tick what is flagged.
  5. A drawer piece by piece: tick the rows, Remove lines.
  6. Earlier days still undecided? Press All.
  7. Read the whole list, then I’ve read the list.
  8. Amber bars: open them. Releases: Send request, then I’ve sent it.
  9. Grey bars stay shut. You are done.
  10. Nothing leaves an estate without every representative’s written approval.
