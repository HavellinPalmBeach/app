### Section 2 · Scope of Services

Havellin Palm Beach will provide the services described in this Agreement and in Exhibit A for the property and its contents, as directed by the Personal Representative. Services include, as applicable: room-by-room documentation and inventory of the contents; sorting, staging, and photographing the contents of consequence; sale, auction, and liquidation coordination; charitable donation logistics with each charity's receipt; disposal and waste management; delivery of items to the persons the Personal Representative names in writing, against signed receipts; and vendor coordination throughout. **The coordination of professional appraisals is not within this engagement** and remains the responsibility of the Client and the estate attorney, who receive Havellin's list of the items it believes warrant one. Where applicable, property preparation is included as reflected in Exhibit A.

The documentation scope for this engagement is **Inventory with values**: the contents list plus an estimated fair market value at the valuation date, with the basis of each value stated.

All disposition decisions — including sale, auction, donation, or disposal of any item — require written authorization from the Personal Representative (each of them, where there is more than one) prior to execution. The specific scope, estimated hours, and projected costs are detailed in Exhibit A (attached estimate), which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

**2.1 Explicit Exclusions**

The following are outside every Havellin engagement and cannot be added by Change Order: legal advice, legal representation or probate court filings (the estate attorney's); tax preparation or filing; management of financial accounts; real property sales (Havellin’s principals, Anthony Graziano and Ashley Jerome, are licensed Florida real estate sales associates with Douglas Elliman and would earn a commission on any listing; brokerage is a separate agreement with Douglas Elliman, this engagement is not conditioned on it, and choosing another broker changes nothing about the Services or the fee); and structural repairs, renovation, construction or any other work requiring a Florida contractor license (where Exhibit A includes property preparation, Havellin coordinates licensed trades, who contract with and bill the Client directly).

### Section 5 · Authority, Probate & Legal Compliance

**5.1 Client Representations**

By signing this Agreement, the Client represents and warrants the following:

1.  The Client has the legal authority to authorize the services described in this Agreement over the contents to be handled, and will identify in writing any items owned by others or held outside the estate, and any exempt property claimed.
2.  If acting as personal representative (executor): where already appointed, the Client holds Letters of Administration; where not yet appointed, the Client is the nominated personal representative and will provide the Letters before work begins, and no work begins until they are on file.
3.  The Client will promptly provide Havellin with any court orders, restrictions, or legal limitations affecting the disposition of estate assets.
4.  The Client will notify Havellin immediately of any disputes among beneficiaries, legal holds, or changes in legal status that may affect the scope of work.
5.  Nothing will be sold, donated, disposed of or released to anyone until each Personal Representative has authorized it in writing as Section 5.3 provides.

**5.2 Working with the Estate Attorney**

Havellin works alongside the estate attorney and does not replace them. Specifically:

1.  Havellin will prepare the Court Inventory of the tangible personal property at the property, each item with its recorded value and the basis for it, for the Personal Representative to adopt and counsel to serve and file under Florida Statute §733.604. Havellin's values are estimates unless an appraisal is noted against the item.
2.  Professional appraisals are arranged by the estate attorney. Havellin will give the appraiser access to the property and be present for the visit on request.
3.  All asset sales will require written Personal Representative approval.
4.  Havellin will keep a record of each disposition it carries out, for inclusion in the estate's final accounting.
5.  All project files will be retained for a minimum of 7 years from project close date.

**5.3 Asset Disposition Authorization**

Havellin will carry out the following at the direction of the Personal Representative, subject to the approvals below; where there is more than one, the written approval of each is required:

**5.4 No Purchase by Havellin; Referral Fees**

Havellin, its owners, team members and contractors will not purchase or otherwise acquire any of the contents of the property, or any other tangible personal property of the estate or the trust, whether directly or through any other person, and will take no part of the proceeds of any sale of that property. Havellin never receives or holds the proceeds of a sale: the selling vendor pays them to the estate.

Havellin may receive a referral fee from a vendor it recommends. Any such fee is paid by the vendor from its own revenue; it does not increase the commission or charges the Client pays, it is never added to the vendor’s invoice, and none is received on the sale of a firearm. The Client may engage any vendor of the Client’s choosing. Where a vendor that sells the contents pays such a fee, the Estimate says so on that vendor’s line and the close-out record of sales names the vendor.

**6.2 Havellin Liability Limits**

Havellin’s responsibility for loss of or damage to an item of the property’s contents caused by its negligence while the item is in its care, custody or control is measured by the item’s documented value: the appraised value where an appraisal has been obtained, otherwise the value the Client declared for it in writing before it was moved, otherwise the value recorded for it on the Project Records (Section 7.1); and in no case exceeds $\[per-item limit\] for any one item or $\[per-occurrence limit\] for any one occurrence, the limits of the coverage stated in Section 6.1.

An item whose documented value exceeds the per-item limit is, before it is moved, either scheduled with Havellin’s insurer for this engagement on an agreed-value basis or handled and transported by a specialist vendor carrying its own coverage; Havellin names those items to the Client before work begins. The Client is responsible for removing or securing jewelry, cash, negotiable instruments and original legal documents before work begins; any such item Havellin finds later is handed over the same day under Section 7, and Havellin’s responsibility for it is limited to that hand-over.

Havellin is not liable for: pre-existing damage to property or items; loss or damage resulting from Client instructions that Havellin advised against in writing; items removed from the property by family members or third parties without Havellin's knowledge; or digital assets, cryptocurrencies, or financial instruments not physically present and accessible at the property.

**6.3 Damage Incident Protocol**

1.  Havellin team member discovers or causes damage — document immediately with photographs and written description
2.  Notify Client / Personal Representative within 24 hours of discovery
3.  Preserve damaged item in place — do not move, repair, or dispose of without Client authorization
4.  Submit written incident report to Anthony Graziano within 24 hours
5.  Engage insurance carrier if damage exceeds $500
6.  Provide Client with insurance carrier contact and claim process within 48 hours

### Section 7 · Confidentiality & Privacy

All information encountered during this engagement — including but not limited to property contents, asset values, financial records, legal documents, family matters, and estate details — is strictly confidential. Havellin agrees to:

-   Engage every team member and contractor under a written agreement carrying confidentiality obligations, executed before they work on any Havellin engagement
-   Not disclose any information about the Client, property, estate, or family to any third party without written consent, except to counsel and others the Personal Representative authorizes, to vendors to the extent needed to perform the Services, to Havellin’s technology providers (cloud storage, and software, including artificial intelligence, used to identify and research items from their photographs) under their confidentiality terms, or as required by law
-   Not post or publish content related to the property or estate on social media or any public platform, except as expressly authorized under Section 7.2 below
-   Maintain all Client and estate records in secure storage with access limited to authorized Havellin personnel only
-   Return the Client’s own documents (deeds, financial and legal papers Havellin encounters) to the Client or counsel, against a signed receipt, and never destroy them; an original will found at the property is handed, unopened, to counsel (or the Client where no counsel is recorded) the same day. Havellin’s Project Records are retained for seven years under Section 7.1

The Client acknowledges that Havellin may use anonymized, non-identifying project data (e.g., square footage, job type, general scope) for internal business analysis and planning purposes only.

**7.1 Documentation Media and Project Records**

Havellin photographs the property, its contents, and all significant assets (these photographs, the “Documentation Media”) as a necessary part of the Services — for condition documentation, asset identification, the §733.604 inventory, appraisal support, and the chain-of-custody record. This documentation is not optional and is not separately charged; it is how the estate is accounted for.

Documentation Media is stored in the estate’s own project folder on Havellin’s Google Drive, accessible only to Havellin personnel assigned to this engagement and to anyone the Personal Representative authorizes in writing — counsel, the appraiser, the court. It is retained for seven years as part of the fiduciary record. It is never sold or licensed. It is not shared with any third party except a vendor performing Services on this engagement, Havellin’s technology providers as Section 7 provides, or as required by law or court order, and is not published except as Section 7.2 permits.

Havellin also maintains a written record of the estate’s contents and their disposition: an itemized list of the property documented, its location and condition, the disposition authorized for each item, the date it was carried out, the person who authorized it, and, where an item was released to a beneficiary or to a third party, the name of that recipient and the reference of any receipt obtained under Section 5.3 (together with the Documentation Media, the “Project Records”). Where an item was sold or consigned and proceeds were actually received, the Project Records state the amount received, any fees deducted, and the net amount. At the close of the engagement Havellin delivers the Disposition Ledger, the Project Records’ final statement of where every item went, to the Personal Representative for review and signature.

The Project Records are Havellin’s work product, prepared for the Personal Representative and forming part of the fiduciary record of this engagement. They are held on the terms stated above and are retained for seven years. Havellin will provide them to the Personal Representative, to counsel of record, and to any other person the Personal Representative authorizes in writing, and will produce them as required by law or court order. This obligation survives completion of the engagement, and the Project Records are not Client documents for the purposes of the return provision in Section 7. Section 7.2 does not apply to the written record, and no part of it is used for marketing or promotional purposes; Documentation Media may be used only as Section 7.2 permits.

**What the Project Records are and are not.** The Project Records are Havellin’s contemporaneous record of work performed at the direction of the Personal Representative. They are not an appraisal, and any value recorded in them is Havellin’s estimate, with its basis stated, unless an appraiser’s report is noted against it; they are not the inventory required by Fla. Stat. §733.604, and they do not evidence that any distribution satisfied a devise or bequest or that title to any item has passed. Responsibility for the estate’s inventory, accounting and distributions rests with the Personal Representative and counsel.

### Section 8 · Termination

**8.1 Termination by Client**

The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time with written notice. Upon termination:

-   Havellin will cease all work and secure the property upon receipt of written notice
-   The fixed fee is earned in stages against the payment schedule in Section 3.3. The deposit is earned in full on signature of this Agreement and is not refundable. Seventy-five percent (75%) of the fixed fee is earned once the project midpoint defined in that schedule is reached. The full fixed fee is earned on completion of the scope of work described in Exhibit A.
-   The Client remains responsible for the portion of the fixed fee earned as of the termination date, together with any third-party vendor commitments the Client has made, or authorized in writing, that cannot be cancelled without charge
-   No part of the fee is calculated from hours worked. The fixed fee does not vary with the time Havellin spends, on termination or otherwise
-   Where Havellin is in material breach of this Agreement and has not cured that breach within seven (7) days of the Client's written notice describing it, no further portion of the fee is earned from the date of that notice, and Havellin will refund any amount it holds above the portion earned before it
-   A final invoice stating the portion of the fixed fee earned will be issued within 10 business days of termination
-   Havellin will provide the Client with all documentation completed through the termination date

**8.2 Termination by Havellin**

Havellin may suspend or terminate this Agreement with written notice under the following circumstances:

-   Non-payment of any invoice more than 30 days past due
-   Discovery of conditions posing a health or safety risk to Havellin personnel that the Client is unwilling or unable to remediate
-   Client misrepresentation of authority, scope, or material facts at intake
-   Court order or legal hold preventing Havellin from completing the contracted scope

On termination by Havellin, Section 8.1 applies as of the date of Havellin’s notice. If the Client ceases to hold the authority under which this Agreement was signed (Letters revoked, a successor appointed, or the Client’s death or resignation), work stops; this Agreement continues with the successor fiduciary once they assume it in writing, or otherwise ends under Section 8.1.
