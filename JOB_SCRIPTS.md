# Six jobs, scripted

*Havellin Palm Beach. Rewritten 2026-10-19 as one page per job (the 2026-10-13 draft is in git history). Dummy data throughout.*

## What we are solving for

Six made-up jobs, one per service, each run from the first call to the final payment through the app's real buttons, by a person. The point is to find anything that would embarrass us on a real job: wrong money on a document, the wrong party or wording on an agreement, a record that gets lost, a job that gets stuck with no way forward. That is all. The rooms, the scores and the exact taps are not the point.

**Pass:** the job reaches its end, every document says what its checkpoints below say, the figures add up to the cent, and nothing met the *fix now* bar. All six pass twice running before anything new is built. Write every finding in the tracker (`WORKFLOW_AUDIT_2026-09-28.md`) under the run's heading: the job, where you were, what you pressed, what happened, what you expected.

## How to run one

- The brown band on the client's dashboard always names the next step and holds its button. Follow it. Intake, the estimate, approval, send, the yes, the agreement, the deposit, activate, the midpoint, close, the final.
- The house is worked on the phone in field mode (press **📱 Field** in the header): the Job Plan tab, room by room, following *Photographing a House*. Everything shot in the house arrives on the laptop's Job Admin & Inv tab, where it is named, valued, decided and papered.
- On every device set *Who uses this device* in Settings first. Sign the laptop into the Havellin Gmail: every email the app makes is a draft you read and send yourself, then press **✓ I've sent it**.
- **The rooms:** tick the rooms the house has. Score each as you see it (1 empty to 5 packed; the fullness chips above the grid set a starting point for all of them). Mark a room ✕ only when the client is clearing it themselves: ✕ prints on the client's estimate as *Excluded, you will handle this space*. On an estate, mark nothing ✕.
- Facts in the tables below must be entered exactly: they drive the documents and the gates. Anything not in a table, do as you would on a real job.
- Write down roughly how long each stage took and anything that felt like too many taps on a full house. That feeds the *fix if cheap* list.

When both runs pass with nothing in *fix now*: the mock job in a real house, then the attorney's hour with the trust package.
